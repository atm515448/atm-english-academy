Privacy Policy / Manufofin sirri

ATM English Academy · Published by ATM Digital Solutions & Services · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the ATM English Academy dictionary uses, and why. We keep the information it uses to a minimum.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or school.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not record the words you type in the search box.

2. Information stored on your device

The dictionary saves these on your phone or computer only:

  • your settings (theme, text size, voice language, examples)
  • the words you saved to My Words
  • the words you marked as learned, and your quiz progress
  • the daily word reminder setting and today's quiz answer

This information stays on the device. It is removed when you use "Erase my saved data" in Settings, or when you clear the browser's site data.

3. Anonymous usage analytics

To help us improve the dictionary, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics. It records these anonymous events:

  • which pages you open (for example Search, Browse, Vocab, Learn)
  • which dictionary words and topics you open
  • which vocabulary view you choose, and when you swap the direction (English to Hausa or Hausa to English)
  • how many letters a search has, and whether the search matched a word (the words themselves are not recorded)
  • whether you install the app to your home screen, or choose "Not now"

Firebase also collects general technical details, such as the type of device and browser, the app version, the language and an approximate country, as described in Google's own privacy policy. This data is not linked to your name, phone number or email.

4. Offline use

After the first visit, the dictionary works without internet. The dictionary files are saved on your device. Analytics events wait until you are online again.

5. Online services

The only outside service the dictionary contacts is Google Firebase, for the analytics described in section 3. The WhatsApp and phone links on the About page open only when you tap them.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can delete the dictionary's stored information with "Erase my saved data" in Settings, or by clearing the browser's site data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. Security

The settings and progress in section 2 stay on the device. Connections to online services use secure HTTPS links.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
